A.R.C. (Artists Resepcting Community) is a movement that began in 2014. It was inspired by a meeting that the Honorable Minister Louis Farrakhan had in Chicago with prominent members of the Hip Hop community from all over the country. In that meeting the minister urged rappers to incorporate more consciousness into their music, end senseless beefs, and be an example that the community can benefit from in a positive way. Dr. Abdul Haleem Muhammad, (the Southwest Student Regional Minister of the Nation of Islam), and other student Ministers  in attendance were instructed by Min. Farrakhan to take what they had witnessed in that meeting back to their respective cities and do the same. Houston rapper K-Rino was called upon by Dr. Abdul Haleem Muhammad to assist in this effort by reaching out to local music people in Houston to participate, and shortly after, the first A.R.C. meeting was held. Pioneers of the movement consisted of veteran artists, producers, label owners, and DJ's such as the Legendary O.G. Wickett Crickett, Ganxsta Nip, Cl'Che, Fiya the Media Mogul, Zin, Murder One, Mr. Cap, and K-Rino. The inital goal was to educate artists on the BUSINESS of music and how to protect their art from a record industry that only intends to enrich ITSELF, not it's artists. A.R.C. also encourages ownership and asks artists to release more positive content to add balance and combat the high quantity of negative music that dominates radio, TV, and social media This mission is also accompanied by consistent community efforts such as feeding the homeless and less fortunate, clothing drives, neighborhood clean-ups, conflict resolution efforts, speaking at schools and more. A.R.C. is also geared towards becoming an organization that people can reach out to when they have questions or need information about aspects of the music industry, and community issues as well. The A.R.C. has released 2 full albums since the start of our movement. "Time to Rise", "Ready for the Revolution" and our 3rd project "Sieze the Time" is expected to be released near the end of 2026. We beleive in the saying that states "With knowledge comes great responsibility" and for 12 years we have worked to make a mark in our city, hoping with God's approval, we can make it better in all aspects.
